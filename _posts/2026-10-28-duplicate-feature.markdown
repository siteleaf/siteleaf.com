#### You can now duplicate anything in Siteleaf (page, post, or document) with a single click.

Writing this month’s newsletter? Start with last month’s. Adding another project to your portfolio? Give an existing one a second life. Sometimes the best starting point is something you’ve already made.

Duplicate it, make your changes, and carry on.

This has been a popular request. It’s a small addition, but one that should save a lot of copying and pasting. Thanks to everyone who asked (we're also [collecting feedback](/blog/a-new-version-of-siteleaf-is-coming) for the next version of Siteleaf!).
